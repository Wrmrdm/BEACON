01

The problem

What expanding across a border actually costs you today

One sale into a new country pulls in a bundle of advisors. You assemble it yourself, brief each one separately, and hold the whole thing together by hand.

  • A domestic tax advisor who knows your books, but not the destination.
  • A foreign tax advisor you have to find, vet, and brief from scratch.
  • A commercial lawyer for contracts, terms, and local consumer rules.
  • An insurance broker for the exposure you've just taken on.
  • And the founder's time to coordinate all of it — the line no one invoices.

Five relationships, five hourly rates, and still no single view of whether you are actually compliant.
