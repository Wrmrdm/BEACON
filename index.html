01

The problem

What one cross-border sale actually sets off

There is no off-the-shelf answer here. The rules are real, country-specific, and badly connected. A single decision — ship from a German warehouse, cross a sales threshold — can trigger a chain of obligations in a system you've never touched.

  • A €10,000 line Cross the EU-wide distance-selling threshold and you charge each country's VAT rate, not your own.
  • Stock abroad Hold inventory in a German fulfilment centre and OSS no longer covers you — you need a local VAT number.
  • A second tax office German registration runs through one Finanzamt, in German, on its own forms and timeline.
  • A new filing rhythm Monthly advance returns via ELSTER, an annual return, and an EC sales list — each with its own deadline and penalty.
  • A law that isn't tax Before you sell to German consumers you must register packaging under the Verpackungsgesetz. Miss it and listings get pulled.

Today you solve this with a domestic accountant, a foreign tax advisor, a lawyer, and a lot of founder time. Five invoices, and still no single view of whether you're actually compliant.

The difference

Knowing a rule exists is not the same as getting it done

Your Europe and the national portals are correct and current. They're also directories. Here is the same obligation, two ways.

"You may need to register for VAT in another member state."

You hold stock in Germany, so OSS doesn't cover it. Register with Finanzamt Kleve. Here is the form, filed, and the date your number is expected.

"Returns are filed electronically."

Your German advance return is monthly via ELSTER for the first two years. The next one is due on the 10th. We prepare and submit it.

"Packaging obligations may apply."

Selling to German consumers requires a LUCID packaging registration first. It's done, with your registration number on file.

"Distance sales may be reported via the One-Stop-Shop."

Your Belgian sales go through OSS from your NL registration — one quarterly return, no Belgian filing. Already set up.
